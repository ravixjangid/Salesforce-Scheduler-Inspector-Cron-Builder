# Privacy Policy

Salesforce Scheduler Inspector & Cron Builder is a local productivity extension.

## What the extension accesses

- Salesforce pages that you are already logged into
- Chrome storage for extension preferences, cached scheduler snapshots, and launcher position
- Salesforce session cookies only for supported Salesforce domains, so the extension can query scheduler data for the org you choose
- Chrome notifications, only when enabled by the browser, to alert you about failed, missed, or stopped schedules

## What the extension stores

The extension stores data locally in your browser:

- selected Salesforce org
- timezone and filter preferences
- cached scheduler snapshots for faster loading
- notification deduplication state
- launcher position

## What the extension does not do

- It does not send your data to any external server owned by this extension.
- It does not upload Salesforce data outside your browser.
- It does not read page content for non-Salesforce websites beyond rendering the Cron Builder launcher UI.
- It does not sell or share your personal data.

## Third-party services

The extension calls Salesforce REST endpoints for the orgs you explicitly access in Chrome. No additional analytics, ads, or external tracking services are included.
